Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

Wallet Accountant ("the App") is a cash wallet tracking application. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App collects, uses, and shares.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App works entirely offline. All your financial data (wallet balances, transactions, budgets, denominations) is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

  • No account or sign-up required
  • No cloud storage or sync
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, or microphone

2. Advertising

The free version of the App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain data to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General location (country/region level)
  • App usage and interaction data

This data is collected and processed by Google under their own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

Pro users who purchase the ad-free upgrade are not shown any ads, and no ad-related data is collected for them.

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time Pro purchase to remove ads. All purchase transactions are handled by the Google Play Store. We do not collect or store any payment information.

4. Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using:

  • SQLite database (transactions, denominations, currencies)
  • SharedPreferences (settings, preferences)

If you uninstall the App, all local data is permanently deleted.

5. Data Export

The App allows you to export your data as CSV or JSON files. These exported files are saved to your device and are under your control. We do not have access to exported files.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
